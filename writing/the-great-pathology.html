The Great Pathology
頑疾

We do not create matter or life — we merely transform them from one form to another. But we do create ideas, which are not bounded by the laws of this physical world.

我們並不創造物質或生命，我們只是將它們從一種形式轉化為另一種形式。但我們的確創造觀念，而觀念不受這個物理世界法則的約束。

Just as material things never truly endure, ideas never truly die, because they are not of this world of death. As long as there are minds to think them, they grow, multiply, mutate, and never reach a final form. Software, a shadow cast from the sky of living ideas onto the land of dying materials, a bastard child of Dionysus riding in Apollo’s chariot, it too lacks a final form — it can grow, multiply, and mutate, never reaching a final form. But since it takes root in the material world, it decays and inevitably dies. Yet unlike material things, which decay by losing content, software decays by gaining it. It does not wither like a plant. It metastasizes like a cancer. New features beget new features, bug fixes beget new bugs. Software grows without a terminal shape, until either the host can no longer sustain it, or it is surgically removed before that happens.

正如物質事物從不真正恆久，觀念也從不真正消亡，因為它們不屬於這個終有一死的世界。只要還有心靈去思索它們，它們便會生長、繁殖、變異，永遠達不到最終的形態。軟件，是活的觀念之天空投射在必死之物質之地上的影子，是狄俄尼索斯的私生子乘著阿波羅的戰車；它同樣沒有終極形態，可以生長、繁殖、變異，永遠達不到最終的形態。但因為它紮根於物質世界，它便會腐朽，終將死去。然而與物質事物的腐朽不同，物質因失去內容而腐朽，軟件卻因增添內容而腐朽。它不像植物那樣枯萎，它像癌症一樣擴散轉移。新功能催生新功能，補丁催生新的漏洞。軟件會不斷生長，直到宿主再也無法承受它，或者在那之前就被外科手術般切除。

Where is the software? Is it in the patterns of voltage in silicon? The magnetic domains on spinning platters and spooling tapes? The trapped charge in flash memory, the light pulses in optical fiber, the radio waves in the air, the pits burned into plastic, the electron spin states, the DNA base sequences? Or is it in the electrical signals in your neurons, or the billions of matrix multiplications in an LLM — which are, again, found in the patterns of voltage in silicon? No, none of these. These are mere incarnations. Software has a soul, and it is not of this material world — it is pure idea.

軟件究竟在哪裡？是在硅片中電壓的樣式裡嗎？是在旋轉的磁盤和卷繞的磁帶上的磁疇裡嗎？是在閃存中被困的電荷、光纖中的光脈衝、空氣中的無線電波、燒錄在塑料上的凹坑、電子的自旋態、DNA的鹼基序列裡嗎？還是在你神經元中的電信號裡，又或者在大語言模型裡數十億次矩陣乘法之中，而這些說到底，又還原為硅片中電壓的樣式？不，都不是。這些都只是化身。軟件有一個靈魂，它不屬於這個物質世界，它是純粹的觀念。

But we are bound to this fleeting material world, and so are our brains, our devices, and our technologies, including AI. Of course, we can always build machines that can process larger and larger codebases. But we as a species are still stuck in the wet, carbon-based, embarrassingly limited brains we were born with. This is not by any means a unique challenge to software: law, mathematics, science, philosophy — any field that builds and maintains a coherent conceptual system across generations must confront the same reality and contain unbounded creative force through formal clarity. Their solution is to develop dedicated semi-formal or fully formal languages. Software, with its formal notation, already sits squarely at the more rigorous end of the spectrum — however, our daily conversations about software, conducted in natural language, fall back into a comfortable denial of this reality, and we let the underlying primal force of growth and death, intoxication and anguish, ecstasy and despair take over.

然而我們被束縛在這個轉瞬即逝的物質世界之中，我們的大腦、我們的設備、我們的技術，包括人工智能，也同樣如此。當然，我們總能造出可以處理越來越龐大代碼庫的機器。但作為一個物種，我們仍被困在與生俱來的那副潮溼的、以碳為基礎的、侷限得令人尷尬的大腦之中。這絕非軟件獨有的困境：法律、數學、科學、哲學，任何需要跨越世代構建並維繫一套連貫概念體系的領域，都必須面對同樣的現實，用形式化的清晰去駕馭那股無邊的創造之力。它們的解法是專門的半形式化乃至形式化語言。軟件憑藉其形式化的記法，本就穩穩地處在這一光譜中更為嚴謹的一端。然而，作為軟件工程師，我們日常用自然語言談論軟件時，卻退回到對這一現實的自我安慰式否認之中，任由生長與死亡、沉醉與苦楚、狂喜與絕望這股底層的原始力量接管一切。

We are doomed. The loss of coherence and comprehensibility is always looming on the horizon of every project the way death looms for every living thing. So we seek an elixir — a new language, a new paradigm, a new technology — as a way out of this misery. But there is no way out.

我們在劫難逃。連貫性與可理解性的喪失，始終籠罩在每個項目的地平線上，如同死亡籠罩著每一個生靈。於是我們尋求一劑靈藥：一門新語言、一種新範式、一項新技術，試圖藉此逃出這場苦難。但根本無路可逃。

As Silenus said — “What is best of all is utterly beyond your reach: not to be born, not to be, to be nothing. But the second best for you is to die soon.” We could refuse to build what existing software can already do, reject gratuitous feature requests, or kill projects before they metastasize — and we should, but this is a hopeless endeavor: the will to life won’t let us have a moment of peace. It compels us to create, grow, expand, multiply — because our whole existence depends on it. And so we create, grow, expand, multiply, all the way into the horror.

正如西勒諾斯所說：“對你而言，最好的事情根本遙不可及：不曾出生，不曾存在，歸於虛無。而次好的事，便是儘早死去。”我們本可以拒絕構建現有軟件早已能做到的東西，拒絕無謂的功能請求，或是在項目擴散轉移之前將其扼殺，我們也理應如此，但這終究是徒勞之舉：求生的意志不會讓我們有片刻安寧。它驅使我們創造、生長、擴張、繁殖，因為我們整個的存在都仰賴於此。於是我們不斷創造、生長、擴張、繁殖，一路走向那恐怖的深淵。

If we must confront the pathology, defeat is prewritten. Knowing this, there are only two paths towards the inevitable end: we could take the easy path of comfort, idleness, and resignation, giving in to the hypnotizing dissolution, the heat death of software; or we could take the bitter path of clarity, discipline, and dignity — architecture, design patterns, clean code — and walk, with humility, acceptance, and determination, into the dark.

倘若我們必須直面這一頑疾，那麼失敗早已寫定。明白了這一點，通向那不可避免之結局的道路便只剩兩條：我們可以選擇安逸、懈怠與聽天由命的輕鬆之路，屈服於那令人昏昏欲睡的潰散，也就是軟件的熱寂；也可以選擇清晰、剋制與尊嚴的苦澀之路，也就是架構、設計模式與整潔代碼，懷著謙卑、接納與決心，走入那黑暗之中。

nur als ästhetisches Phänomen ist das Dasein und die Welt ewig gerechtfertigt.

— Nietzsche, Die Geburt der Tragödie

唯有作為一種審美現象，人生和世界才永遠是合理的。

— 尼采，《悲劇的誕生》
