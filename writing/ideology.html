On Order, Authority, and Domination
论秩序、权威与支配

I. Order and Human Difference
一、秩序与人的差异

  1. Human beings are embedded in an order they did not create and cannot ultimately defy. They are creatures before they are creators: they did not originate their own existence, nor their capacity to create.

    人被置于一种自己并未创造、也终究无法违抗的秩序之中。在成为创造者之前，人首先是被造之物：人既没有创造自身的存在，也没有创造自身所拥有的创造能力。

  2. A good life is impossible in a bad society, and a good society cannot be composed of people living badly. The individual and the collective are inseparable.

    在败坏的社会中不可能有良善的生活，良善的社会也不可能由过着败坏生活的人构成。个体与共同体不可分割。

  3. Human beings are unequal by nature—radically different in ability, disposition, strength, and weakness. These differences precede every political hierarchy.

    人在天性上并不平等—在能力、性情、长处与短处上存在根本差异。这些差异先于一切政治等级而存在。

  4. These differences give rise to forms of hierarchy that precede political institutions. Political hierarchy, by contrast, is constituted through human relations and institutions.

    这些差异产生出先于政治制度而存在的各种等级关系。与之不同，政治等级则通过人与人之间的关系及制度而形成。

II. Authority and Obedience
二、权威与服从

  1. Political authority cannot be justified simply by appealing to hierarchy. Its authority rests upon obedience. What can be disobeyed is not sacred merely because it has been established.

    政治权威不能仅仅以等级的存在来证明自身的正当性。它的权威建立于服从之上。可以被违抗之物，不会仅仅因为已经建立起来，就因此成为神圣之物。

  2. Neither domination nor obedience is a virtue.

    支配与服从都不是德性。

  3. Political power is not distributed at random. Human beings are drawn toward power, status, comfort, security, and deference, while institutions create incentives that favor particular kinds of people.

    政治权力并非随机分配。人容易趋向权力、地位、安逸、安全与他人的服从，而制度所建立的激励机制，则会偏向特定类型的人。

  4. Political systems therefore tend to reward those least restrained by virtue and most driven by these impulses, while demanding restraint and obedience from those they rule.

    因此，政治体制往往奖赏最少受德性约束、最受这些冲动驱使的人，同时要求被其统治的人保持克制与服从。

III. The Self-Perpetuation of Domination
三、支配的自我延续

  1. So long as these impulses remain coupled to institutions that reward them, domination remains possible.

    只要这些冲动仍与奖赏它们的制度相结合，支配就依然可能存在。

  2. Violent revolution does not alter this by itself. It can replace those who hold power while leaving the incentives and culture through which power operates intact.

    暴力革命本身并不能改变这一点。它可以更换掌权者，却使权力赖以运作的激励机制与文化保持不变。

  3. The same is true of abolishing a particular form of power. If the impulse remains, it will find another vehicle.

    废除某一种权力形式也是如此。如果冲动仍然存在，它便会寻找另一种载体。

  4. Eliminating domination therefore requires transforming the spiritual and cultural conditions that let it perpetuate itself, not merely rearranging institutions.

    因此，消除支配，需要改变使支配得以不断自我延续的精神与文化条件，而不仅仅是重新安排制度。

IV. Creation and Transcendence
四、创造与超越

  1. The drive toward power should be cultivated, not suppressed. Without it, we become content to submit—to convention, to institutions, to those who claim authority, to false idols.

    对权力的驱力应当得到培育，而不是被压制。没有这种驱力，我们便会甘于服从—服从惯例、制度、那些自称拥有权威之人，以及虚假的偶像。

  2. But power need not be sought over others. The drive can be sublimated—its energy turned upward toward creation, discovery, excellence, and transcendence.

    但权力未必需要通过支配他人来获得。这种驱力可以被升华—将其力量向上引向创造、发现、卓越与超越。

  3. In creating, human beings glimpse the source of their existence and their capacity to create. They encounter something outside and prior to themselves, and are forced to recognize the conditions in which they have been placed.

    人在创造之中，得以窥见自身存在与创造能力的源头。他们遭遇某种外在于自身、又先于自身之物，并被迫承认自己所处的存在条件。

  4. Natural hierarchy need not be abolished. What must be eradicated is domination and obedience, which confuse human power with true authority and human arrangements with true order. Without a true conception of order and authority, we cannot know what makes a good life or a good society.

    天然的等级无需被消除。必须消除的是支配与服从，因为它们混淆人的权力与真正的权威，也混淆人为的安排与真正的秩序。若没有对秩序与权威的正确认识，我们便无法知道什么造就了良善的生活，也无法知道什么造就了良善的社会。
