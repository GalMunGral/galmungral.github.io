On Order, Authority, and Domination
论秩序、权威与支配

  1. Human beings find themselves within an order they did not create and cannot ultimately escape. They are creatures before they are creators: they did not make themselves or the power by which they create.

    人发现自己身处一种并非由自己创造、也终究无法逃脱的秩序之中。在成为创造者之前， 人首先是被造之物：人没有创造自己，也没有创造使自己能够创造的力量。

  2. Human beings exist both as individuals and as members of a whole. Neither can be understood apart from the other.

    人既作为个体而存在，也作为整体的一部分而存在。二者无法彼此割裂地理解。

  3. What constitutes a good life therefore cannot be separated from what constitutes a good society. The two rise or fall together.

    因此，何为良善的生活，不能脱离何为良善的社会来理解。二者共同兴衰。

  4. Yet human beings are not alike. They differ greatly in capacity and disposition, in talent, temperament, strength, and weakness. Such differences belong to the human condition itself, not to the political order imposed upon it.

    然而，人与人并不相同。他们在能力与性情上存在巨大差异，在才能、气质、强弱与长短上各不相同。 这些差异属于人的存在本身，而非政治秩序强加于人的产物。

  5. Political hierarchy is something human beings construct within this prior order. Its authority is not intrinsic to the hierarchy itself; it depends upon obedience.

    政治等级是人在这一先在秩序之中建构出来的东西。它的权威并非等级本身所固有，而取决于服从。

  6. What depends upon obedience cannot claim sacred authority in itself. Political power has only the authority that people continue to grant it by obeying.

    依赖服从而存在之物，本身不能宣称拥有神圣的权威。政治权力所拥有的权威，只及于人持续以服从赋予它的权威。

  7. But obedience and power do not arise independently of human desire. People are drawn toward power, status, comfort, security, and the submission of others.

    但服从与权力并非独立于人的欲望而产生。人容易趋向权力、地位、安逸、安全以及他人的服从。

  8. Political institutions give these impulses structures through which they can be pursued and rewarded. They therefore do not merely distribute power; they help determine who will seek it, who will obtain it, and how it will be exercised.

    政治制度为这些冲动提供了追逐与获得奖赏的结构。因此，制度所做的不只是分配权力； 它也在塑造谁会追逐权力、谁会获得权力，以及权力将如何被运用。

  9. A system that rewards the pursuit of power can elevate those least restrained by virtue, while placing obedience and restraint upon those beneath them. In this way, power can corrupt those who wield it and subordinate those who live under it.

    一个奖赏权力追逐的制度，可能使最少受德性约束的人居于高位，同时要求处于其下的人服从与克制。 如此，权力既可能腐化掌权者，也可能使生活在其下的人处于从属地位。

  10. So long as the impulses and institutions that sustain this pattern remain, domination remains possible. Changing its outward form does not necessarily change what produces it.

    只要维系这一模式的冲动与制度仍然存在，支配就仍然可能。改变其外在形式，并不必然改变产生它的根源。

  11. A revolution may replace one ruling group with another without changing the underlying game. The same is true when one vehicle of power is abolished: the impulse can simply take another form.

    革命可以用另一个统治集团取代原有的统治集团，却不改变背后的博弈。同样，废除一种权力的载体， 也未必能消除背后的冲动；冲动只会采取另一种形式。

  12. Domination therefore cannot be ended by rearranging institutions alone. What must change is also the human material that gives those institutions their power.

    因此，支配不能仅靠重新安排制度来终结。必须改变的，也包括赋予这些制度力量的人自身。

  13. The alternative is not to suppress the forces that drive people to strive, compete, and excel. It is to give those forces another direction.

    出路并不是压制驱使人奋斗、竞争与超越的力量，而是为这些力量指向另一条道路。

  14. They can be directed toward making and discovering, toward excellence and transcendence, rather than toward acquiring power over others.

    这些力量可以被引向创造与发现，引向卓越与超越，而不是用于取得对他人的权力。

  15. Creation turns the human being outward. In creating, we encounter both the source of our existence and the source of our capacity to create. We discover something that precedes us and exceeds us.

    创造使人转向自身之外。在创造之中，我们同时遭遇自身存在与创造能力的源头， 并发现某种先于我们、又超越我们的存在。

  16. The aim is not to erase human difference or ambition, but to free them from the struggle for domination. What is sought is a form of freedom that does not require mastery over others, and a humility that remembers that neither our existence nor our creative power began with us.

    目的并不是抹去人的差异或抱负，而是使它们摆脱对支配的争夺。所追求的是一种不以支配他人为条件的自由， 以及一种始终记得自己的存在与创造之力并非始于自身的谦卑。
